# Nexus Sushi Bar — Restaurant Design Brief

## Concept

A futuristic neighborhood sushi and tea bar where cyberpunk energy meets Zen hospitality. The restaurant is a calm refuge inside a luminous coastal city: customers come for carefully prepared sushi, Japanese tea and a moment of mindful pause.

## Visual direction

- **Cyberpunk layer:** midnight indigo architecture, neon cyan and magenta signage, violet reflections, holographic menu details and restrained glowing accents.
- **Zen layer:** natural wood, bamboo, stone, bonsai, water, warm paper lanterns, quiet geometry and deliberate negative space.
- **Food and tea:** carefully presented sushi, ceramic teaware, steam and subtle preparation animations.
- **Tone:** welcoming, contemplative and cozy; not dystopian, aggressive or visually noisy.

## Buddhist-inspired treatment

The theme should communicate mindfulness, balance, compassion, simplicity and intentional hospitality. Use broad Zen-inspired design cues such as a small rock garden, water, lotus as a botanical motif, tea ritual and calm service. Do not use sacred figures, mantras, or religious iconography as generic decoration or commercial branding. If explicit Buddhist symbols or teachings are ever proposed, pause for the user's direction and ensure respectful context.

## First playable restaurant slice

1. Transition from the completed dive to the restaurant at night.
2. Transfer caught fish into ingredient stock.
3. Generate one customer order at a time.
4. Serve sushi using matching fish stock or tea using tea-leaf stock.
5. Earn coins and reputation for correct service.
6. Show a shift summary and allow the next dive.

## Initial menu

- Nigiri Zen — sardine
- Nigiri Aurora — clownfish
- Nigiri Neon — blue tang
- Matcha Sereno — matcha tea
- Hojicha Noturno — roasted tea
- Chá Lótus — floral infusion

## Interaction and accessibility

- Large tap targets for phones and tablets.
- Portrait and landscape layouts.
- Clear distinction between sushi and tea categories.
- Order, ingredient stock, reward and feedback always visible.
- No time pressure in the first slice; customer patience can be introduced after the core loop is stable.
