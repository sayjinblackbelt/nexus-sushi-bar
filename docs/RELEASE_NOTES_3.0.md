# Nexus Sushi Bar 3.0.0

## What's new

- Five explorable biomes: Blue Lagoon, Neon Reef, Lotus Abyss, Emerald Forest and Thermal Vents.
- Twelve fish species with biome-specific habitats and progression unlocks.
- Fishing progression expanded to level 7.
- Daily and weekly rotating challenges with automatic rewards.
- Activity milestones at 3, 5 and 7 active days. Breaks do not erase earned progress.
- Recurring VIP nights, Lantern Festival and Astral Tide guest event.
- Three restaurant atmospheres: Neon Nexus, Emerald Garden and Golden Lanterns.
- Save schema 13 with backward-compatible normalization.
- Portuguese, English and Spanish interface foundations retained.

## Quality status

Automated validation is required before release. Manual gameplay review on mobile and desktop remains outstanding; visual details, text overflow and touch interactions should be checked on real devices.

## Technology

HTML5 Canvas, CSS, native JavaScript ES modules, LocalStorage and GitHub Pages.
