# Nexus Sushi Bar — Roadmap

## Product direction
A cozy pixel-art game built around two complementary rhythms: daytime underwater exploration and fishing; nighttime sushi preparation and restaurant management. Balanced difficulty means light resource management, not punishing failure.

## V0.1 — Underwater prototype
- Canvas-based pixel-art inspired scene.
- Keyboard and touch movement.
- Five fish species with different values and movement speeds.
- Proximity-based catch interaction.
- Oxygen drain and surface return.
- Eight-slot catch basket.
- Local persistence for coins.

### Acceptance criteria
- Start from title overlay.
- Diver moves in four directions and stays inside the play area.
- Fish swim and can be caught at close range.
- Catching is blocked when no fish is nearby or the basket is full.
- Oxygen decreases during a dive and ends the dive at zero.
- Returning to the surface converts catch into coins.
- Coins persist after refreshing the page.
- Touch controls work on mobile browsers.

## V0.2 — Restaurant night
- Day/night transition.
- Restaurant scene and customer queue.
- Sushi recipes based on collected fish.
- Order preparation and serving.
- Revenue, reputation and daily summary.

## V0.3 — Progression
- Diving gear upgrades.
- Larger basket and oxygen tank.
- New ocean zones and fish.
- Recipe discovery and restaurant upgrades.

## V1.0 — Release candidate
- Original sprite sheets and animation polish.
- Sound effects and ambient soundtrack.
- Tutorial and accessibility pass.
- Responsive layout and mobile QA.
- Save data versioning and balancing.
